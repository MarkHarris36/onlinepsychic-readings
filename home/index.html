GOOD TO KNOW

A few common questions

How do I choose a reading topic?

Start with the question you are asking today. You can use the question finder above or browse love, career, tarot, astrology, dreams and life path guides.

Does the question finder send my words to anyone?

No. It matches words in your browser and keeps your question only in this browser session. It does not submit your question to us.

Are the sign guides live predictions?

No. Our zodiac and horoscope guides explain themes and give you ways to reflect. They are not personalized or real-time predictions.

What happens if I choose a partner reading?

You leave this site for a third-party provider. Review its terms, privacy practices and current offer before sharing personal details or paying.
